Cotes is a calm, minimal notes app. Open it and start writing — that's it.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics.
• Notes live only in the app's private storage on your phone.
• Nothing goes to cloud backups or device-to-device transfers — your notes leave the phone only when you share them or save a backup file yourself.

<b>Writing without friction</b>
• Autosave while you type — there is no Save button.
• Instant search across titles and text, with matches highlighted.
• Swipe right to pin a note on top, swipe left to delete — with Undo.
• Share a note to any app, or share text from any app to Cotes to make a new note.
• "New note" shortcut on the app icon.

<b>Make it yours</b>
• System, light or dark theme, plus pure black for AMOLED screens.
• Five accent colors or your wallpaper colors (Material You).
• Text size for the editor, compact list, text previews.
• English and Russian, switchable right in the app.

<b>Your data, your files</b>
• Back up all notes to a single .json file and restore them on any phone.

Works on Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets (list and note side by side).
